## Evidence and limits

The editor's local `media understand`/`media inspect` pipeline supplied source
hashes, contact sheets and requested/decoded presentation timestamps. The calling
image-capable agents inspected the selected contact sheets; the concept scout
also inspected native-resolution frames around the connector/tag reveal and
persisted attributed observations with `media observe`. No remote model or
upload was used in this study. Sparse pictures show state changes, not exact
original easing curves or continuous playback. Native preset defaults are
editable authored choices rather than a claim to have recovered hidden layers.

Task artifacts live in `outputs/motion-library-expansion` beside this checkout:
the source session, title/badge detail inspections, attributed observations,
`scout-later/README.md` and its source-bound node/quote/checklist evidence. Keep
these receipts with actual native demo exports and their inspection records.

Quote/attribution builds around 345–348 s and a staged checklist around 425–429 s were
also observed during scouting. They remain candidates for a later focused study;
they are not new components in this change.

## Use and grow

The three pattern skills are discoverable through `playbook skills` and
`playbook recommend --task compose`. Each points to its component contract;
`editor-motion-recipes` coordinates their use and the shared catalog's bounded
same-canvas mix. Search `motion catalog --mixable`, then choose the appropriate
entry and replace demonstration content. See [motion recipes](motion-recipes.md).

Use [the mixed native example](../examples/21-reference-motion-mix.tsx) to explore
one shared palette and staggered focus. Its editable title, milestone, concept
links and counter demonstrate composition; it is not the source's business story.
Render and inspect the actual export, preserve readable holds and sources, and
scope future reference preferences to their intended project rather than making
one creator's choices universal.
